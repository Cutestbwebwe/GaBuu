About GaBuu 关于我们

Our story · 我们的故事

🌺

Brand Story Photo

More Than Just Flowers
不只是一束花

GaBuu was born from a simple belief — that flowers are the most beautiful language of love. Founded in Kuala Lumpur, we are a boutique floral atelier dedicated to creating handcrafted bouquets that carry genuine emotion.

GaBuu诞生于2023年的七夕节，在加影创立，是一家精品花艺工作室，专注于打造充满真挚情感的手工花束。

From the finest fresh blooms sourced daily to our signature knit bouquets that last a lifetime, every creation at GaBuu is made with precision, artistry, and heart.

从独特的针织永生花束，到我们每日精心挑选的新鲜花材，GaBuu 的每一件作品都是用心、用艺、用爱打造的。

Delivery FAQ 常见问题

What are your delivery hours? 配送时间是？

We deliver from 9am to 7pm daily, including weekends and public holidays. 我们每天上午9时至晚上7时配送，包括周末和公假。

Can I schedule a specific delivery time? 可以指定配送时间吗？

Yes! You can select morning (9am–12pm), afternoon (12pm–3pm), or evening (3pm–6pm) delivery slots. 可以！您可以选择早上、下午或傍晚的配送时段。

What if the recipient is not home? 如果收花人不在怎么办？

Our driver will contact the recipient before delivery. If unreachable, we'll reschedule at no extra cost. 司机会在送达前联系收花人，如无法联系将安排重新配送，不收额外费用。

Do you deliver on public holidays? 公假也送吗？

Yes, we operate year-round including public holidays. However, orders during festive periods (CNY, Hari Raya, Valentine's Day) should be placed 3–5 days in advance. 是的，我们全年运营包括公假。但节日期间（农历新年、开斋节、情人节）建议提前3-5天下单。

⚠️ For last-minute or urgent orders, please contact us directly on WhatsApp and we'll do our best to accommodate you.

如需紧急订单，请直接通过 WhatsApp 联系我们，我们将尽力安排。
